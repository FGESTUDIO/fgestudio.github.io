For Website 2.0 work, also read:
- `docs/PROJECT_STATE.md`

When actively developing:
- use a `claude/<task-or-release>` branch;
- never overwrite another AI's unmerged work or force-push shared branches;
- treat the Website 2.0 master plan as vendor-neutral product direction, not ChatGPT/Codex-specific instructions;
- do not reintroduce graphic design as an active Website 2.0 business line unless explicitly instructed;
- do not present software/app development as an official FGESTUDIO business line unless explicitly instructed;
- prefer mature reusable solutions when appropriate, but verify license/security/compatibility;
- update `docs/THIRD_PARTY_REUSE.md` for external reuse;
- before handing work to another AI, update `docs/CURRENT_HANDOFF.md` with the exact branch and commit plus tests and remaining work;
- follow the repository-specific safety and product rules in `AI_HANDOFF.md`.
